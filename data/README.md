# Daily off-campus analysis data
off_campus_routes.csv contains 327 rows and 29 columns from sheet 2 of the team’s Off Campus routes.xlsx workbook. Date order, numerical values and missing values are retained. WorkDate is the observation date; 1A/2B/6A/6B are route ridership measures; remaining columns describe weather. Credit: the Purdue–CityBus research team and CityBus of Greater Lafayette.
